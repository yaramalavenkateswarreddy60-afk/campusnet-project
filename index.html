Guaranteed Invariants:

  • Allocation[i] ≤ Demand[i] (Demand Bound)

  • Work-conserving: Unused headroom is iteratively reallocated to high-priority unmet flows.

1. Mathematical Formulation of WFQ-PF

Let $C$ be the total shared gateway capacity, and for each campus segment $i$, let $D_i$ be requested demand and $w_i > 0$ be its priority weight.

Phase 1: Guaranteed Slices

$$A_i^{(1)} = \min(D_i, M_{\text{min}, i})$$

Phase 2: Progressive Filling of Remainder ($R = C - \sum A_i^{(1)}$)

For each unsatisfied segment in active set $S$:

$$\Delta_i = R \times \frac{w_i}{\sum_{j \in S} w_j}$$

If $\Delta_k \ge (D_k - A_k)$, segment $k$ is saturated to its demand, removed from $S$, and the unused surplus is iteratively redistributed among remaining hungry flows.

2. Metric Definitions & Integrity

  • Capacity (Mbps): The configured maximum physical or modeled channel rate.
  • Demand (Mbps): The offered transmission rate requested by campus hosts.
  • Allocation (Mbps): The bandwidth assigned to the class queue by the QoS scheduler.
  • Throughput (Mbps): The rate actually delivered and observed via measurement tools (e.g. iperf3). Modeled allocation is never conflated with measured throughput.
  • Utilization (%): $\frac{\text{Allocation}}{\text{Capacity}} \times 100$. Handled safely when capacity is zero.
